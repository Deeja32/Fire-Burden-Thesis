# The Cost of Residential Fires in Australia: Code and Data

Supporting files for the UNSW Honours Thesis

- **Author:** Daniel Bui
- **Supervisor:** A/Prof Shaun Chan

---

The model estimates the **annual national cost of residential fires in Australia** from 2017-18 to 2023-24 using the **human capital approach (HCA)**. It has three components:

| Component | Method | Main outputs |
|---|---|---|
| Mortality | Value of lost life (VLL): discounted market plus non-market production, weighted by age, sex and state | Expected loss per death $L$; annual mortality cost |
| Morbidity | Years lived with disability (YLD) for burns (INTEGRIS-burns) and smoke inhalation (GBD), valued at annual production | Annual morbidity cost |
| Direct costs | Hospital costs (NHCDC cost per separation × AIHW separations) plus fire service response costs (ROGS) | Low, central and high direct cost by year |

A **willingness-to-pay (WTP)** comparison, using the OIA value of a statistical life and value of a statistical life year, is reported alongside the HCA headline.

**Conventions**
- All dollar values are in **2024-25 Australian dollars**.
- Years are **financial years** unless stated otherwise.
- Costs per separation are in **dollars per separation**.
